Neon Cascade · bomb design preview

  • 5+ in a row: the gem you moved turns into a bomb (in a cascade, the middle gem of the run).
  • Bombs also fall in rarely: about 1 in 12 new gems, at most 4 on the board.
  • Match 3+ bombs: each one blasts the 3×3 square around it, and bombs caught in a blast go off too.
